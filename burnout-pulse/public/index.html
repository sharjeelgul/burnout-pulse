How the score works

Each answer is worth 0 to 4. The six answers are added and scaled to 0 to 100, so higher means more strain. The bands (0 to 24, 25 to 49, 50 to 74, 75 to 100) are reading aids, not clinical cut-offs. The questions borrow ideas from burnout research (exhaustion, detachment, reduced sense of accomplishment), but this is not the Maslach Burnout Inventory or any other validated instrument, and it can't diagnose burnout, depression, or anything else.

Turn on the live AI coach?

The live coach is an AI. To reply, it sends each message you type, your recent coach conversation, and your latest scores to Anthropic for processing. Your name and email are never sent. Messages that trigger the crisis check are never sent.

If you leave it off, you get offline reflection prompts that run entirely on this device. They are simple prompts, not AI.
